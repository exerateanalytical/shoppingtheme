Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How do I know your peptides are genuine and pure?

Every Alluvia product comes with a third-party Certificate of Analysis (COA) from an ISO-accredited laboratory, including HPLC purity data, mass spectrometry confirmation, and lot number traceability. You can download the COA directly from each product page.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We ship to most countries worldwide via cold-chain courier services. International orders include insulated packaging with phase-change cooling media. Please check local import regulations before ordering — peptides are legal for research purposes in most jurisdictions.

How should I store my peptides?

Lyophilised (freeze-dried) peptides should be stored in a freezer at -20°C in their sealed vials, away from light and moisture. Once reconstituted with bacteriostatic water, refrigerate at 2–8°C and use within 30 days. Never refreeze reconstituted peptide.

What is your return and refund policy?

Due to the nature of peptide products, we cannot accept returns on opened vials for safety reasons. However, if your product arrives damaged, incorrect, or if there is a documented quality issue, we will replace or refund at no cost. Contact us within 7 days of delivery.

How do I reconstitute a lyophilised peptide?

Use bacteriostatic water (BAC water) for reconstitution. Inject the BAC water gently along the side of the vial — do not inject directly onto the peptide powder. Swirl gently; never shake. Each product page includes a specific reconstitution guide with exact volumes for your desired concentration.

Do you offer wholesale or B2B pricing?

Yes — we work with clinics, compounding pharmacies, research institutions, and resellers. Wholesale pricing, white-label options, and bulk COA documentation are all available. Contact us using the form above and select "Wholesale / B2B Enquiry" as your subject.
